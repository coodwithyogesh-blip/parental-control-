# Permissions & privacy

- Only the configured Telegram numeric owner ID is allowed to interact.
- Keep the Telegram bot token private.
- Android OS permissions cannot be bypassed.
- Location retrieval is a sensitive action; use only on a device under your authority and with appropriate notice/consent.
- Camera, microphone, SMS harvesting, clipboard collection, and contact extraction are not remotely implemented.
- Use visible, legitimate parental-control setup and follow applicable laws and Android policies.
